About these results

Every number on this page comes from the Marathon County Clerk's official election reports. On election night the page updates itself about once a minute as new county reports are posted — no need to refresh.

Results are unofficial until they are certified by the county board of canvassers in the days after the election. Precinct counts show how much of each race has been tallied, and a leading candidate is marked ✓ only after every precinct in that race has reported.

In a Wisconsin partisan primary, each voter chooses one party's ballot, so races are counted and shown by party — use the tabs to switch between them. Referendum questions are listed under their own tab.
